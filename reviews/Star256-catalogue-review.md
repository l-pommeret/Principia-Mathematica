# ✱256 source catalogue review

The 30 existing `PM-VERBATIM` blocks in `Star256Source.lean` are catalogued once, in source order, as six homogeneous batches of five. The canonical witness is the 1913 Volume III scan; Volume III has no Project Gutenberg witness in the campaign registry. Exact item pagination is deliberately left pending rather than inferred from neighbouring ✱255 records.

Every source ID has a homonymous declaration in `Star256OpeningKernel.lean` or `Star256MiddleKernel.lean`. These are only nominal typed mappings: several declarations are reflexive restatements, abstract pass-throughs, or substantial weakenings of the printed ordinal-series claim. Consequently all 30 items remain `prepared` and `semantic-audit-pending`; this catalogue promotes none.

The deterministic PM parser does not yet cover the Volume III ordinal/cardinal operators used by this section. Every item therefore has the explicit `reviewed-gap` parser route.
