The ✱111 catalogue uses cardinal/type constructions that exceed the current
propositional PM parser.  These records are explicitly parser-reviewed while
their diplomatic readings and typed Lean witnesses remain independently
audited.

The current rejected forms are deliberate and localized:

- `✱111·1`, `·11`, `·112`, `·13`, `·131`, `·451`, and `·452` use the
  unimplemented `sm` / `sm sm` similarity syntax in proposition position;
- `✱111·14`, `·12`, `·121`, and `·15` use relation restriction `↾`;
- `✱111·18` and `·221` use the cardinal construction `†`;
- `✱111·02`, `·21`, `·23`, and `·211` use incomplete-symbol image or
  correspondence notation without a parser context;
- `✱111·01` contains the hatted relation-building definition, while `·201`
  combines contextual existence with nested correspondence/similarity syntax.

By contrast, `✱111·16` is accepted by the current parser and therefore has no
`reviewed-gap` exemption. Parser status is independent of the strict semantic
promotion verdict.
