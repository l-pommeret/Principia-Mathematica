# Q280 PM-derivation migration — ✱11·311, ·32, ·33, ·34, ·341

None of these five items currently qualifies for `pm-derivation-v1`.
✱11·311 is source-only and depends solely on the likewise blocked
metalinguistic conjunction rule ✱10·13.

The four definitions in `Star11Q280Kernel.lean` retain exact `Raw` targets for
·32, ·33, ·34, and ·341. Their former structures were not derivations: they
stored polymorphic suppliers of unary ✱10 certificates, sometimes a reflexive
counter `2 = 2`, and `target = target`. No field related an input assertion to
the displayed two-binder output through the PM judgement relation. Those
structures and their canonical-looking constructors have therefore been
removed.

Dependency graphs were rebuilt from zero. The printed direct edges are exactly
the bracketed citations: ✱10·13; ✱10·27; ✱10·271; ✱10·27·28; and
✱10·271·281 respectively. Since only source text or target constructors remain,
there is no actual PM theorem call: every Lean and normalized dependency list
is empty. Composite printed labels are preserved rather than silently expanded.

The blocking requirement is a typed rule that lifts an existing assertion
derivation beneath a second apparent binder and composes the result to the
specific `Raw` endpoint. Until such a rule is derived from the PM kernel, these
items remain `prepared` and blocked.
