# PM I ✱52 opening semantic audit

The canonical 1910 text and the local `PM-VERBATIM` transcription agree for
✱52·01, ·1, ·11, ·12, and ·13.  Classes are represented extensionally by
membership predicates, `ιʻx` by the singleton predicate, `1` by the class of
such predicates, and `Dʻι` by the domain of the singleton relation.

All five Lean conclusions preserve the printed quantification and both
directions of every equivalence.  In ✱52·12, `E!(ℙx)(φx)` is represented by
existence and uniqueness of a satisfying argument, which is precisely the
condition for the extension of `φ` to be a unit class.  No inhabitance,
choice, finiteness, or weakened implication is added.

The proofs of ✱52·1 and ·11 close directly from the typed definitions; their
printed citations are retained in metadata as audited historical dependencies.
