# PM II ✱104 catalogues 03–04 strict semantic audit

These two audits cover ten new canonical items, five per lot, while skipping
the already catalogued ✱104·12.

Catalogue 03 promotes only ✱104·121.  Its two premises and conclusion are
represented exactly by `Asc s t`, `Asc2 s u`, and `Asc t u`; symmetry followed
by transitivity proves precisely the printed descent.  The four other theorem
types merely unfold `Asc` or `Asc2` and omit explicit type restrictions or
specialized cardinal equalities.

Catalogue 04 promotes ✱104·122, ·123, and ·13.  The first is the extensional
form of ·12/·121.  For ·123, the printed dependency ✱103·26 normalizes its
cross-type cardinal equality premise to the same typed equinumerosity relation.
The existential used by Lean for ·13 is exactly the domain characterization
of `N¹C`.  By contrast, Lean ·14 is an identity theorem with none of the
printed existential membership conditions, and Lean ·141 is an image
injectivity result unrelated to cardinal closure under ascent; both are
refused.
