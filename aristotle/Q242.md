> ✱5·1. ⊢ : p . q . ⊃ . p ≡ q
>
> I.e. two propositions are equivalent if they are both true. (The statement

>
> ✱5·32. ⊢ :. p . ⊃ . q ≡ r : ≡ : p . q . ≡ . p . r

> I.e. to say that, on the hypothesis p, q and r are equivalent, is equivalent
